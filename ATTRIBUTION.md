# Image attribution

Every sky, planet, nebula, galaxy, and asteroid texture in this project is a real astronomical image. None of them are AI-generated illustrations.

## Sky

- `assets/space_panoram.jpg.jpg` and `assets/textures/sky/great_observatories.jpg` — NASA Great Observatories mosaic produced for the International Year of Astronomy 2009 (Hubble, Spitzer, and Chandra). NASA images are public domain.
- `assets/textures/sky/milky_way.jpg` — Solar System Scope Milky Way star map, based on NASA imagery, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

## Planets and Sun

- `earth.jpg` — NASA Visible Earth / Blue Marble (`land_shallow_topo_2048`). Public domain.
- `mars.jpg`, `jupiter.jpg`, `saturn.jpg`, `moon.jpg`, `sun.jpg`, `neptune.jpg`, `venus.jpg`, `saturn_rings.png` — [Solar System Scope](https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/) equirectangular maps based on NASA elevation and imagery, CC BY 4.0.

## Nebulae

- Orion Nebula — Hubble Space Telescope mosaic (NASA / ESA / M. Robberto).
- Horsehead Nebula — Hubble infrared view (NASA / ESA / Hubble Heritage Team).
- Pillars of Creation — James Webb Space Telescope NIRCam (NASA / ESA / CSA / STScI).
- Helix Nebula — Wide Field Imager / ESO and Hubble data.
- Lagoon Nebula (M8) — Hubble / NASA.
- Carina Nebula — Hubble mosaic (NASA / ESA).
- Veil Nebula — Hubble / NASA.

Thumbnails were retrieved from Wikimedia Commons.

## Galaxies

- Andromeda (M31) — optical + H-alpha composite (public astronomical survey imagery via Wikimedia Commons).
- Whirlpool Galaxy (M51) — Hubble optical image (NASA / ESA).
- Sombrero Galaxy — NASA Great Observatories composite (Chandra, Hubble, Spitzer).

## Asteroids

- 243 Ida — Galileo spacecraft (NASA / JPL).
- 433 Eros — NEAR Shoemaker (NASA / JPL / JHUAPL).

Solar System Scope textures require the CC BY 4.0 credit above. NASA / ESA / CSA / STScI / ESO stills are used under their published image-use policies (NASA public domain; ESA/Hubble typically CC BY 4.0).

## Font

- `assets/fonts/DejaVuSans.ttf` — DejaVu Sans (Bitstream Vera / public-domain changes).
